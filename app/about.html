About Nearest Jisho

Nearest Jisho is an offline Japanese-English dictionary. It finds words even when the search has a typo, a missing long vowel or a doubled letter, and it shows results while you type. Everything runs on your device; the dictionary data is stored by your browser after the first visit.

How search works

  • Type English, Japanese (kana or kanji) or romaji. Romaji is matched against readings, English against meanings.
  • Results come in groups: Japanese words, English meanings, Slang and Wiktionary, Names.
  • When nothing matches exactly, the nearest spelling is shown with the reason (for example "letters swapped").
  • Verb and adjective forms such as 食べた or 食べさせられた lead to the dictionary form.
  • Tap a result to open its full entry: every meaning and example, other forms, the kanji in the word, and words that start with it. The X button in the search box clears the search.
  • Click the star on a word to keep it in Favorites (star button next to the settings). Favorites and recent searches are stored on this device only.
